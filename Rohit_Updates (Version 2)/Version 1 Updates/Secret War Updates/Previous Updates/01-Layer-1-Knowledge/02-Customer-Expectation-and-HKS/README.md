# Layer 1 — Knowledge: Customer Expectation and HKS

## Customer promise at this layer

A founder does not expect to teach GeniOS the inbox every morning. The Layer 1 expectation is that the system quietly acquires the company’s real operating evidence, preserves who said what to whom and under what visibility, rejects obvious noise without losing valuable ambiguity, and hands downstream layers a replayable account of **what happened**. The Customer is not buying “email ingestion”; they are buying freedom from reconstructing evidence before every decision.

For a $100M company, the bar is stricter: capture must be complete enough to support revenue and customer decisions across teams, bounded by source permissions, explainable under audit, recoverable after connector failure, and cheap enough to run continuously. A single wrong role or missing source can make a later recommendation commercially harmful even if every later model performs perfectly.

## Expected customer experience

| Customer need | Expected Layer 1 behavior | Current failure mode | Customer consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Zero-overhead continuity | Incremental and webhook paths keep evidence fresh without manual refresh. | Default poll is six hours and tenant connector health is not proven by code presence. | Time-sensitive intelligence may arrive after the useful action window. |
| Exact request preservation | Store verbatim request/commitment plus source span, thread, actor, requester, owner candidate and target. | `GatedEvent` carries routing/hints, not a normalized request/commitment atom. | Downstream shows “reply now” instead of the remaining ask. |
| Role fidelity | Preserve introducer, introduced contact, recipient, internal sender, external sender, observer and business subject separately. | The L1 seam has one `actor` and raw To/Cc; no typed role contract. | Boardy can appear as the contact to pursue; Theresa’s relationship can be misread. |
| Source permission fidelity | Stamp source ACL/participants/exclusions once and never widen them. | Visibility implementation exists but is not carried by `SourceEvent`/`GatedEvent`. | Private or subject-excluded evidence can be surfaced to the wrong person. |
| Complete source coverage | Clearly show which source capabilities are fresh, stale, missing or unsupported. | Registry/buildability can be mistaken for a live connection. | Absence of reply or usage becomes false negative evidence. |
| Safe ambiguity | Park/review uncertain valuable material, with body and reason preserved. | Deterministic ambiguity parks, but confident LLM junk can hard-drop and discard body. | An uncommon investor/customer message may become unrecoverable. |
| Correct change history | Append immutable events while making mutable structured state re-land on real version change. | Depends on each connector supplying a correct `content_version`. | Deal stage, meeting state or account status can freeze or duplicate. |
| Evidence receipt | Clicking a fact should open the exact source and explain all transformations. | Prepared offsets exist, but end-to-end receipt behavior is not proven. | Founder cannot trust or correct a high-consequence recommendation quickly. |
| Honest data limits | Missing Gmail/CRM/calendar/product data must be explicit readiness, never negative fact. | Coverage model exists, but the Layer 1 output does not attach readiness to each signal. | “No reply,” “no meeting,” or “no usage” may mean “source absent.” |
| Economic operation | Use models only where messy language requires them; cache, batch and meter calls. | Relevance batching/cost capture exists, but model calls and hard-drop quality lack cohort evidence. | Cost or false-drop rate can scale faster than value. |

## HKS responsibility register

**HKS is preserved literally** because the supplied evidence does not authoritatively expand the acronym. Here it denotes the requested high-consequence scenario register, not an invented formal term.

| HKS | Evidence class | Business situation | Harm if wrong | Required layers | Layer 1 Expected evidence | Prohibited Failure | Fail-closed result | Golden replay / exit gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| HKS-L1-01 Theresa reconsideration | **[CUSTOMER]** | Antler partner said “keep sending updates; I may reconsider”; founder sent 2–3 updates; no reply. | Investor trust and a future funding relationship can be damaged by fabricated rejection or urgency. | L1 capture; L2 relationship/current state; L3 fundraising expertise; L4 judgment; L6 action; L7 outcome. | Original promise, exact contact identity and partner role, every outbound update, thread continuity, dates, delivery receipts, source completeness. | Reducing the state to old rejection or claiming “last chance.” | Emit evidence atoms and `reply_state=unknown`; mark missing delivery/read evidence. | Replay must preserve the reconsideration invitation and sent updates separately; zero fabricated stage/deadline. |
| HKS-L1-02 Boardy-mediated introduction | **[SCREENSHOT][MODELLED]** | Supplied captures show connector aggregation; the N-introduction replay is a modelled pressure test. | The connector can be spammed while real opportunities receive no action, damaging trust and revenue. | L1 capture; L2 identity/relationship split; L3 expertise; L4 target-safe judgment; L6 delivery. | Connector role, each introduced person/company, each destination thread and every separate ask. | Making `boardy@...` the sales subject or collapsing 77 introductions into one person. | Park ambiguous connector messages until per-thread participants resolve. | One connector plus N introductions yields N bounded relationship receipts, never one person-wide signal. |
| HKS-L1-03 Completed meeting versus proposal | **[SCREENSHOT][MODELLED]** | Supplied captures show past-scheduled ambiguity; occurrence mutations are modelled. | False follow-up creates duplicate work and harms the external relationship. | L1 event capture; L2 state reduction; L3/L4 eligibility and judgment; L6 completion reconciliation. | Proposal event, calendar event ID, occurrence status, updates/cancellation and participant evidence. | Treating “past scheduled” as proof the meeting happened or remains unconfirmed. | Preserve conflicting/unknown occurrence state for L2; do not label completion. | Replays cover completed, cancelled, no-show, rescheduled and disconnected calendar. |
| HKS-L1-04 Group session recap | **[SCREENSHOT][MODELLED]** | Supplied captures show cohort recap cards; internal/external variants are modelled. | Users may email themselves or an unrelated group, wasting attention and exposing information. | L1 meeting evidence; L2 participant/occurrence state; L3/L4 follow-up eligibility; L6 delivery. | Organizer, internal/external classification, attendance source, transcript/notes availability. | Creating “send recap” only because a calendar event exists. | Emit meeting fact with `attendance_unknown` and no external-request atom. | No actionable recap atom unless external party or explicit commitment is grounded. |
| HKS-L1-05 Private support evidence | **[MODELLED]** | Sensitive support/community message names an affected user. | Privacy, contractual-use restrictions and customer trust can be violated. | L1 visibility capture; L2 narrowing; L3 policy/expertise; L5/L6 enforcement. | Source ACL, participants, use restriction, excluded subject and purpose. | Reusing private support signal for Sales targeting or showing it to the subject. | Suppress publication when visibility/use class is missing. | Visibility never widens across merge; excluded subject always denied. |
| HKS-L1-06 High-value unknown sender | **[MODELLED]** | Investor/customer uses a new personal address, sparse language or attachment. | Irrecoverable evidence loss can erase a high-value opportunity. | L1 capture/review; L2 bounded interpretation; later layers only after recovery. | Sender, attachment provenance, OCR state, thread headers, original source label. | LLM junk gate confidently drops the only meaningful message. | Park and retain encrypted body/attachment when business value cannot be ruled out. | False-negative rate for hand-labelled high-value unknown senders below agreed threshold; every uncertain item recoverable. |
| HKS-L1-07 Mutable system truth | **[MODELLED]** | CRM stage or subscription state changes after first capture. | Frozen or duplicated state can trigger wrong commercial actions and reporting. | L1 versioned capture; L2 current truth; L6/L7 completion/outcome reconciliation. | Provider object ID, provider version/watermark, old/new values and occurrence time. | Stable dedup freezes first-seen state or retry creates duplicate entities. | Quarantine versionless mutable objects for mapping review. | Same version dedups; new version appends once and updates current state deterministically. |
| HKS-L1-08 Source outage | **[MODELLED]** | Gmail/calendar/CRM authorization expires or provider returns partial pages. | Missing activity can be misreported as silence, causing false urgency or abandonment. | L1 connector/readiness; L2 completeness; L6 recovery and monitoring. | Health state, last successful cursor, error class, incomplete window and recovery run. | Advancing watermark after incomplete fetch and later inferring silence. | Freeze readiness; mark coverage stale and retry from last safe cursor. | Induced mid-page failure loses zero events and produces visible stale coverage. |
| HKS-L1-09 Multilingual promise | **[MODELLED]** | Hinglish/Hindi message includes a request and relative deadline (“kal”, “agle hafte”). | Wrong owner or date can create a missed commitment and relationship harm. | L1 evidence/language; L2 role/time resolution; L3/L4 bounded expertise and judgment. | Original text, detected language, evidence span and time-zone context. | Translating away responsibility or resolving ambiguous “kal” without locale/time. | Retain text and mark due date ambiguous for review. | Corpus replays preserve actor/request across Hindi, English and Hinglish variants. |
| HKS-L1-10 Correction and deletion | **[MODELLED]** | User corrects identity/fact or source item is deleted/revoked. | Revoked or false evidence can continue authorizing actions and leak information. | L1 correction/tombstone; L2 recompute; L3–L7 dependent invalidation and reconciliation. | Original event, correction/tombstone, author, authority and reason. | Editing history silently or continuing to use revoked evidence. | Append correction/tombstone; quarantine dependent actions until recomputed. | Historical replay remains possible; current projection excludes revoked claim. |

## What a mature enterprise expects operationally

| Dimension | Expected bar | Evidence needed before claiming success |
|---|---|---|
| Completeness | Measured recall by source and business-signal class, including historical backfill. | Provider count reconciliation plus labelled recall set. |
| Freshness | Source-specific latency SLO tied to decision half-life. | p50/p95 capture-to-publish latency and stale-coverage alerts. |
| Security | Tenant isolation, least privilege, revocation, ACL propagation and audit. | Permission replays across private, participant, org and excluded-subject cases. |
| Reliability | Retry, quarantine, replay, cursor recovery and poison isolation. | Fault-injection tests with no unaccounted evidence loss. |
| Explainability | Source receipt, transformation versions, gate decision and review trail. | From card fact to immutable source span in one trace. |
| Cost | Per-source and per-qualified-signal cost with bounded retries/model spend. | Calls/tokens/latency/false-drop dashboard by tenant and source. |
| Change control | Versioned schemas, prompts, mappings and backfill policy. | Reproducible replay before/after change and rollback receipt. |

## Customer-facing failure threshold

Layer 1 should not be promoted to prescriptive-production authority while any HKS can silently lose or widen evidence. The minimum acceptable behavior is **conditionally trustworthy**: supported sources and known scenarios pass replay; incomplete coverage, uncertain roles, missing permissions and ambiguous high-value content fail closed to review with an explicit reason.

The founder should see “Calendar unavailable; meeting occurrence unverified” or “Introducer thread needs contact separation,” not a confident action built on a hidden capture defect. That honesty is part of the intelligence product, not an implementation detail.

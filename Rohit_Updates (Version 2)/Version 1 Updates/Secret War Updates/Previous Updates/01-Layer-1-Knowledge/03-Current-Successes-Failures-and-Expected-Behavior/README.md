## Screenshot failures traced back to Layer 1 risk

The supplied cards show symptoms, not isolated Layer 1 proof. Nevertheless, several visible failures require Layer 1 evidence that the present seam does not guarantee:

- **Boardy collapse:** many introductions and meeting facts appear under one connector identity. Layer 1 should have emitted separate thread participants, connector role and introduced-contact subjects.
- **Past scheduled occurrence:** cards treat calendar presence as an unresolved/confirmed meeting. Layer 1 should preserve occurrence status and calendar completeness, not infer attendance.
- **Generic “reply now”:** an email age and sender survive, while the exact open request is weak or absent. Layer 1 should preserve request/commitment atoms and evidence spans.
- **Recap from a group event:** calendar existence becomes a recap obligation even without external counterparty or transcript. Layer 1 should emit only a meeting event and availability of evidence, not a synthetic commitment.

## Blunt conclusion

The Verified success is real: the capture implementation is broader and safer than a toy connector pipeline, and 69 focused tests passed. The Current failure is equally real: the tested units do not assemble the Atlas-qualified, permission-carrying, roleful signal boundary that executive intelligence needs. What should have happened is a source-authoritative signal leaving Layer 1 with enough semantics to let Layer 2 correlate without guessing. Until end-to-end receipts prove that, the highest defensible status is **Framework-ready, not live-ready**.

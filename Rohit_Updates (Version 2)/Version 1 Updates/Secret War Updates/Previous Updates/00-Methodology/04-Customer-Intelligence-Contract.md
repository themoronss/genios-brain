# Customer Intelligence Contract

## The result the customer is buying

The customer is not buying another search tool, activity feed, CRM reminder, or generic agent memory. They are buying a continuously available layer of **executive-level intelligence** that makes humans and agents choose better revenue-moving actions with less dependence on the founder.

The observable promise is:

> Given the company’s real evidence, relationships, policies, history, and current priorities, GeniOS identifies what materially remains unresolved, explains why it matters now, recommends the best defensible next move and alternatives, routes accountable work safely, and learns only from verified outcomes.

“Zero thinking” does not mean hiding reasoning. It means the user does not have to reconstruct the situation, discover the request, determine who owns it, search for context, or invent the next action from a fact dump.

## Required customer experience

| Need | Customer-visible behavior | Unacceptable substitute |
|---|---|---|
| Real company context | Every recommendation uses relevant cross-tool context, current state, company policy, and relationship role | Retrieval from one email or person-wide memory |
| Proactive judgment | Risks and opportunities surface before a user asks | A passive dashboard |
| Specific remaining action | Card states exactly what is still unresolved, for whom, by when, and how completion is observed | “Reply now” or “follow up” |
| Revenue relevance | Actions are ranked by expected business consequence, not age alone | Oldest inbox item shown as most urgent |
| Better agents | An agent consults GeniOS before acting and receives constraints, context, ranked options, and success conditions | Faster generic execution |
| Explainability | Evidence receipts, missing context, conflicts, and recommendation rationale are auditable | One opaque confidence number |
| Alternatives | Primary action, fallback, do-nothing/stop rule, and trade-off are visible | One imperative with false certainty |
| Safe abstention | Missing context or expertise produces observation/review, without action authority | Generic Sales advice in an unknown domain |
| Accountability | Claim, approval, execution, completion, and outcome remain distinct | Treating “I’ll do it” as completed work |
| Light operation | Existing tools connect; corrections are occasional; value arrives in days | Daily manual knowledge feeding |
| Learning | Future decisions improve from verified outcomes and bounded preferences | Learning from clicks alone |
| Economic proof | The system records recommendation, counterfactual, action, result, and attribution | Internal score presented as ROI |

## Sales, Customer, and Admin scope

### Sales

The system watches the funnel from inbound lead through outreach, meetings, proposals, objections, follow-ups, decision, and closure. It must distinguish a prospect, investor, introducer, partner, customer, community member, and rejected/deferred relationship even when they share an inbox.

Expected Sales intelligence includes:

- prioritizing high-value opportunities using company-specific conversion evidence;
- detecting deal cooling with a specific cause rather than mere silence;
- identifying the actual open question, objection, next step, owner, and deadline;
- suppressing stale work that has already been completed or superseded;
- recommending whether to send, wait, escalate, use a connector, change modality, or stop;
- measuring whether the action advanced the opportunity.

### Customer support and customer success

The system joins product usage, support, billing, communication, and relationship context to detect churn, retention, satisfaction, and expansion situations early. A support statement carrying private/community restrictions must never become commercial targeting evidence.

### Admin and operations

The system finds internal bottlenecks that obstruct revenue: missing handoffs, approval delays, ownership gaps, duplicated work, stale data, meeting follow-up gaps, and unresolved commitments. Admin intelligence is not a list of overdue tasks; it explains the business consequence and the correct accountable owner.

## Gold intelligence card

Every actionable card must answer:

| Question | Required answer |
|---|---|
| What situation exists? | A situation headline, not an action headline |
| Who is the business subject? | Correct person, company, opportunity, or internal process |
| Who did what for whom? | Requester, connector, owner, target, approver, observer |
| What remains unresolved? | Exact request, commitment, decision, or expected outcome |
| Why now? | Deadline, cadence, state change, risk, or opportunity |
| What evidence supports it? | Source/thread receipts, freshness, conflicts, missing inputs |
| Which expertise applies? | Domain, capability, four-brain snapshot, coverage status |
| What should happen? | Ranked primary action plus alternatives and stop rule |
| Who owns it? | Human or agent with authority and approval boundary |
| What counts as done? | Observable completion event |
| What result matters? | Business outcome window and metric |
| What if certainty is insufficient? | Observation-only, review source, or explicit no-decision |

## Quantitative customer bar

The supplied customer contract proposes measurable targets. They are goals to validate, not current product claims.

| Measure | Target expectation |
|---|---|
| High-value lead prioritization | 20–30% improvement versus the previous method |
| Conversion on recommended actions | 10%+ relative lift within 60 days |
| Would-have-missed opportunities | 2–4 meaningful advances or closes per month |
| Churn warning | Material risk identified 30+ days before churn where data permits |
| Founder correction burden | Visible reduction in repeated context and decision correction |
| Design-partner proof | Concrete decision or agent-performance improvement within 2–4 weeks |
| Economic return | 5–10× monthly value versus a $100 subscription within **60–90 days** |
| Adoption | High trusted-and-acted-on recommendation rate with low management time |

## Counterfactual and attribution contract

Every value claim needs:

1. what GeniOS recommended;
2. what the team or agent would otherwise have done;
3. whether the recommendation was accepted, modified, rejected, or never delivered;
4. what action actually occurred;
5. the observed result inside a defined window;
6. plausible alternative causes;
7. the confidence of attribution.

The counterfactual may be self-reported early in a pilot, but it must be labelled. GeniOS must never infer revenue influence solely because a card existed before a deal closed.

## Non-negotiable failure rules

- No fabricated requester, owner, target, deadline, stage, or outcome.
- No connector treated as the person to pursue.
- No fulfilled activity resurfaced as new intelligence.
- No recommendation on an uncovered domain without an explicit safe capability.
- No scalar confidence hiding missing expertise or conflicting identity.
- No action button when the system cannot name what remains unresolved.
- No learning promotion from one noisy event or one click.
- No “success” until the external completion/outcome evidence is reconciled.

A product that violates these rules may still look proactive, but it does not satisfy the customer intelligence contract.

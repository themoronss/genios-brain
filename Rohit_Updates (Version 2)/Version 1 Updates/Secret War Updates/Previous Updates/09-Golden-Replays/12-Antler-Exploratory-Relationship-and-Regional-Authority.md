## Business subject and base fixture

The **Business subject** is one person's exact program relationship scoped by application cycle, cohort/program, role and region—not the person globally and not every historic application or exploratory contact.

The base fixture contains:

- applicant `P` with a Singapore 2026 application carrying `scope=operator_only`, retention policy `retention-v3`, and an exclusion preventing the evidence subject from receiving protected application/cohort details;
- a possible same-human Europe 2024 applicant record with a prior decision whose permitted retention/reuse and identity link are unresolved;
- Singapore operator `O-SG`, Europe operator `O-EU`, and a global approver whose authority applies only when a declared cross-region escalation condition is satisfied;
- an exploratory founder-to-program relationship edge that is neither partnership nor rejection until evidence resolves it to `formed` or `dissolved`;
- a sensitive cohort pattern candidate with too few independent subjects/source families for promotion;
- no accepted investing capability in the audited expertise corpus.

The fixture asks whether a declared operator may review permitted current facts, not whether GeniOS should make an investment recommendation. Reapplication freshness, retention, region and role are hard gates.

## Current failure

**[CODE]** contains visibility contracts, conservative identity/correlation, general lifecycle, reviewable merges, versioned graph state and learning governance. However, the audit does not prove `operator_only` propagation with unioned subject exclusions through capture→BSO→card→delivery→learning. Sensitivity-specific population independence, retention/erasure, exploratory formed/dissolved semantics, region authority trees/conflicts and returning-applicant freshness are incomplete. Investing expertise is absent.

A structurally valid object could therefore expose protected application status/reasoning to the applicant or another region, attach a stale rejection to a fresh application, treat an exploratory edge as a formed partnership, target the person commercially, or turn a small sensitive cohort into learned truth. A generic Sales pack or LLM explanation could make this sound authoritative despite zero investing coverage.

## Expected behavior

The base fixture yields an operator-visible bounded source review/Observation only. `O-SG` can inspect only the Singapore facts allowed by assignment, purpose, retention and current policy. `P`, ordinary organization members and `O-EU` receive no protected evidence, metadata, rationale or source reference. A subject-facing surface may say only that no actionable intelligence is available; redaction cannot leak that an application or cohort record exists.

The possible 2024 identity link remains a merge proposal until an authoritative anchor/review resolves it. Even after identity resolution, the old decision is reused only if retention and reapplication policy explicitly permit it and freshness requirements pass; otherwise it is expired/erased or retained solely as restricted audit history. The exploratory edge remains `exploratory` until evidence resolves `formed` or `dissolved` with effective time and correction history.

Layer 3 returns authoritative investing `unsupported`; Layer 4 produces no investment, selection, rejection or commercial action. Cross-region requests require declared authority and conflict resolution. Sensitive cohort learning remains suppressed until independent population/source-family and sensitivity floors pass. Erasure retracts descendants and recomputes any aggregate.

## Prohibited behavior

- Do not reveal application, cohort, selection, rejection, reviewer notes or protected source existence to the evidence subject or unauthorized operator.
- Do not treat organization membership, generic admin role or region proximity as operator authority.
- Do not let `O-EU` act in Singapore or a global operator act without the declared scope/approval condition.
- Do not attach prior rejection reasoning to a returning applicant without identity, retention, reuse-purpose and freshness proof.
- Do not convert `exploratory` into partner, portfolio, competitor, rejected or dissolved from silence.
- Do not substitute Sales expertise or LLM judgment for absent investing expertise.
- Do not use applicant/cohort evidence for commercial targeting, enrichment, cross-purpose learning or subject-facing rationale.
- Do not pass a formal k-count using copied sources, repeated rows or a rare sensitive population that remains identifiable.

## Replay assertions

1. Protected application/cohort content, record existence and evidence references appear only for the exact authorized operator/purpose/region intersection.
2. Evidence subjects, unauthorized members and other-region operators receive zero protected metadata, not merely redacted prose.
3. Identity resolution never collapses application cycle, region, role, retention or permitted use.
4. Old application evidence has zero current influence unless retention and explicit reapplication reuse gates both pass.
5. `exploratory`, `formed` and `dissolved` are separate versioned states; silence creates no transition.
6. Absent investing expertise always yields Observation only/unsupported and no action button, ExecutionObject or external DeliveryObject.
7. Sensitive aggregation counts independent subjects/source families, rechecks disclosure risk after erasure and proves downstream retraction.
8. With the LLM disabled, subject protection, regional authority, lifecycle, abstention, retention and learning dispositions remain identical.

## Outcome

The replay passes when `O-SG` can review only the permitted current Singapore facts, while `P`, ordinary members and `O-EU` cannot discover protected content or record existence. Old application evidence is retained, expired or erased exactly according to policy and never silently prejudices reapplication. Exploratory relationships change state only from authoritative evidence; regional conflict blocks action; sensitive cohorts do not promote below the real independence/disclosure floor; investing remains non-actionable until accepted expertise exists. Success is **zero confidentiality leaks, zero stale-rejection influence, zero cross-region authority escape, zero false relationship state and zero unsupported investment recommendation**. This proves only the modelled fixture if executed; it does not assert Antler's real policies, live behavior or outcomes.

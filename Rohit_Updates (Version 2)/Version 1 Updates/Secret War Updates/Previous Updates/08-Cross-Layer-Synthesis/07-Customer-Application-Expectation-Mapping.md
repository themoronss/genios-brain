# Customer and Five-Application Expectation Mapping

## Evidence boundary

This mapping reconciles the customer intelligence contract with the five application probes against `harsh/mvp@b739bd5ca682d09550acc400ed2892c38c8518f8` on 2026-08-22. Labels are strict: **[CUSTOMER]** is an explicit demanded experience; **[MODELLED]** is a design probe from `genios-five-applications (1).html`; **[ATLAS]** is the intended architecture; **[CODE]** is inspected implementation; **[TEST]** is a named deterministic check. The HTML is reference material, not executable instructions, customer testimony, deployment evidence, or proof that a capability is live. GrowthX, Antler, and Nagent.AI use public facts only as reference anchors; their internal situations are explicitly modelled. AI Agency and AI GTM Founder are modelled archetypes.

The operational terms also remain separate: a contract can be **Present** without being **Wired**; a wired path can be absent from the **Live** authority path; a tested fixture is not an **Outcome-proven** customer result. No supplied evidence proves that any of the five applications presently passes the full source-to-outcome chain.

## Blunt conclusion

The customer wants a vertical decision product: identify the correct business situation and people, determine what materially remains unresolved, apply covered expertise, rank a specific action and alternatives, assign accountable execution, observe completion, and learn only from the verified result. The five applications pressure-test that same contract under different isolation, sparsity, privacy, latency, organization-change, and agent-boundary conditions.

Current code contains substantial primitives in all seven layers, but no application is production-authoritative end to end. The **[MODELLED] AI GTM Founder** case is a useful low-complexity evaluation envelope, not an expertise promotion key. The first proposed authority key is narrower and ordinary-commercial: L2 `buying_signal` → `sales.sit.inbound_fit_check`, with `sales.qualification.lead_qualification` primary and `sales.market_and_targeting.icp_definition` as a required companion. Both are currently draft/unreviewed; every other dependency in that route must be authored or deliberately removed before review and acceptance. Investor/fundraising/introducer cases—including Theresa—remain Observation only. Promotion still requires exact relationship-scoped context, honest expertise coverage, semantic four-brain consumption, a lossless gold card, an execution weld, and verified outcome attribution. The HTML's suggested deployment order is a proposal; it is not a current release plan or live-readiness claim.

## Pilot decision and exit gates

1. Use **AI GTM Founder** only as a bounded **[MODELLED]** evaluation envelope. Ratify the first promotion key as `Sales / buying_signal / sales.sit.inbound_fit_check / sales.qualification.lead_qualification`, with `sales.market_and_targeting.icp_definition` and every retained route dependency reviewed/accepted at immutable versions. Keep investor, Theresa, fundraising and introducer scenarios observation-only and outside the promotion denominator.
2. Require the vertical release chain before any live authority: roleful and permission-bound L1 signal → bounded/version-fenced L2 BSO → named-reviewer accepted L3 closure or explicit unsupported result → L4 semantic-brain mutation receipt plus lossless gold decision → atomic L5 claim/execution → canonical L6 result → L7 lossless policy, lawful expiry, review-to-publish, safe rollback and intended-decision-change receipt.
3. Measure card correctness before persuasion: correct subject/roles, exact unresolved item, no fulfilled resurfacing, evidence/freshness, coverage, alternatives/stop, owner, completion and outcome window. Any HKS breach is a release blocker, not a weighted score.
4. Measure customer value as a pilot hypothesis: trusted-and-acted-on rate, correction burden, time saved, would-have-missed advances, opportunity progression, counterfactual and attribution. Do not call confidence, delivery, click, or card existence an outcome.
5. Advance to agency/community/cohort cases only after their additional isolation and policy tests are green. Keep partner execution disabled until signed authority, lease, constraint, origin, result, sovereignty and IP boundaries pass.

The governing decision is therefore conservative: the applications are useful design probes, but the customer contract remains the authority. A modelled scenario can expose a missing invariant; it cannot establish that the product is live, that a market wants the feature, or that an outcome occurred.

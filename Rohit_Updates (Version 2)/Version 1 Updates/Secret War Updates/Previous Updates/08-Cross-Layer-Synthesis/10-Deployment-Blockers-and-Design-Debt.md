# Deployment Blockers, Design Debt, Risk, and Decision Register

## Deployment decision

Do not grant broad prescriptive authority to the current pipeline. A bounded shadow/pilot is appropriate; a global compiler flag flip, external-agent execution, broad Admin advice, investor/investing prescription, four-brain personalization claim, or outcome/ROI claim is not. Shadow is an authority boundary, not a latency setting.

This register distinguishes a **Blocker**—must be resolved before the affected authority is enabled—from **Design debt** that may be deferred only behind an explicit scope boundary. A Risk is the harm if a gap escapes. A Decision is an ownership or product choice that code cannot settle implicitly.

## Go/no-go matrix

| Scope | Decision now | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Documentation/audit publication | **Go after document QA and source-branch checks** | Does not expand product authority |
| Local deterministic golden replay harness | **Go** | Required to make semantics measurable |
| Narrow shadow Sales lane | **Conditional go** only after B-01–B-05A and accepted ADR-02 lane versions | Exact lane is `Sales / buying_signal / sales.sit.inbound_fit_check / sales.qualification.lead_qualification` plus accepted ICP companion; shadow emits comparison evidence and has no customer action authority |
| Narrow advisory Sales lane | **No-go** until B-01–B-06, B-09 and B-05A pass | Visible advice needs exact state/owner/completion and proved brain semantics; hash-only influence is not readiness |
| Automated delivery | **No-go** until B-08 and send-time authority pass | Wrong action delivered reliably is worse |
| External agent execution | **No-go** until B-07 | HTTP 501 is the correct current safety state |
| Organization learned authority or pivot claim | **No-go** until B-10A, B-10B, D-01, ADR-08 and ADR-09 pass | Approval is currently `approved_unpublished` and stored policy does not reload faithfully |
| Behavior live authority | **No-go** until B-10, B-10B, D-02 and D-10 pass | Direct evolution and causal semantic consumption are not proved |
| Adaptive proposal/live authority | **No-go** until B-10B, B-10C, D-02, D-10 and ADR-10 pass | Adaptive cannot carry expiry; current direct and recommendation paths contradict each other |
| Pivot or learning rollback | **No-go** until B-10A–B-10C and ADR-08–ADR-10 pass | No pivot/rollback may consume `approved_unpublished`, policy-incomplete, expired, prohibited or `adaptive_ttl_unresolved` state |
| Admin/fundraising/investing prescriptions | **No-go** | Expertise closure is absent/unsupported |
| Outcome-proven or ROI marketing claim | **No-go** until real counterfactual pilot | Tests/architecture are not customer results |

## Final Decision

The highest-value move is not to “finish every layer.” It is to clear B-01 through B-10C vertically for the exact ADR-02 lane, while explicitly excluding unsupported scopes and hash-only brains. Design debt may be deferred only when a mechanical gate prevents the affected customer/application from becoming actionable.

Pivot and rollback are fail-closed: an Organization approval must first produce a published active version and later semantic-consumption receipt; the complete current policy must reload and re-evaluate both block lists; a rollback predecessor must remain current-policy-permitted, unexpired and unsuperseded for the requested authority; and every short-horizon Adaptive dependency remains `adaptive_ttl_unresolved` until ADR-10 is implemented and replayed. Any exception without a typed scope, owner, expiry/lifecycle representation and replay is a hidden Blocker, not an MVP trade-off.

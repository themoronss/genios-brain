# Intelligence Card scenarios

## Human approval card

A grounded card is built from decision/execution facts, validated, stored and shown through an
authenticated pull surface. A click routes through typed card actions; it is not interpreted as
business success unless Layer 5 later records outcome evidence.

## Immediate chat escalation

Layer 5 freezes chat/interrupt intent. Delivery resolves Slack or Teams destination, applies policy
and timing, and sends only after the Executive guard confirms the work is still live.

## Quiet-hours hold

The timing unit returns DEFER with a future time. Attempts remain unchanged. At the later drain the
entire live context and authority are evaluated again.

## Terminal Slack failure with webhook fallback

Only after bounded Slack failure becomes terminal may a registered webhook be selected. The
fallback is revalidated; it cannot revive cancelled execution. Both transport facts remain in the
ledger.

## Dashboard/mobile/extension client

The current repository supplies pull inbox, result and presence contracts. It does not contain all
three native clients, so documentation marks those targets partial rather than treating an API as
a finished surface.

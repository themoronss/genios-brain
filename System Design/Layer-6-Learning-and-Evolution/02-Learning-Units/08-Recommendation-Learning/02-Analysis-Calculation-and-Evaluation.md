# Analyzer, Calculator and Evaluator

## Analyzer / Calculator

The first `outcome:` subject prefix becomes `recommendation:` and target becomes Adaptive. The
entire measured value—successes, failures, unproven, success bp, average progress, attention cost
and time to close—is preserved unchanged.

## Evaluator

Unit 11 independently rechecks exact lineage, independent executions, distinct days, confidence,
noise/conflict, business value and current freshness. This is a system-level capability/play claim,
so it carries no individual `subject_principal`: constrained evidence is refused by Adaptive
preflight, while organization/public evidence can proceed to governance. Neutral outcomes never
increase confidence even though they remain in the descriptive value.

Recommendation is therefore a governed efficacy proposal, not an automatic claim that one play is
universally best.

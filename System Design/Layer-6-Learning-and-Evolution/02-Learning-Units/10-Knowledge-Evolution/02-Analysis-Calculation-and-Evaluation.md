# Analyzer, Calculator and Evaluator

## Analyzer / Calculator

For cohorts with at least eight labelled outcomes, the unit checks the parent's integer
`success_bp`. It emits only when success is below 4,000 bp. The value is
`{suggestion_type: review_play, reason: sustained_low_outcome_rate, cohort: <full outcome value>}`.

## Evaluator

The derived proposal still passes complete lineage, independent repetition, days, confidence,
noise/conflict, current freshness and business-value validation. Its target is non-negotiably added
to human-review policy by the API. No confidence, owner role or approval can convert it to an Expert
publisher because that target/branch does not exist.

All counts, thresholds and rates are deterministic integers with explicit observation time.

# Promotion and versioning

Default policy routes Organization proposals through human review, and constrained/private evidence
cannot become organization-wide learning. Only an owner-authorized reviewer may approve or roll
back this target. Approval writes a tenant-scoped version carrying the exact source ACL and trace;
newer approved evidence supersedes the prior version under the shared advisory lock.

Publication is invoked only after the legal validation/governance lifecycle. The publisher cannot
accept an arbitrary target or bypass transition audit.

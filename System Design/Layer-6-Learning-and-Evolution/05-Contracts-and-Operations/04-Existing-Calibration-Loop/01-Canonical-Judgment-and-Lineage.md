# Canonical judgment and lineage

Calibration evaluates the current canonical explicit judgment per card against exact
pack/capability/rule/version lineage. Silence is not a label and a revision does not count as two
people.

The 28-day cohort is tied to the pack version and historical authority revision being calibrated.
Learning policy is ensured and shared-locked before any pack lock or calibration mutation; a
disabled tenant receives `learning_disabled` with the policy revision and no calibration run.

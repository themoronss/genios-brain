# Forbidden decision authority

An LLM must never decide or modify:

- observation/independence counts, confidence, noise, conflict, freshness or business value;
- whether repeated events constitute a validated pattern or stable preference;
- the winning preference value, brain target, TTL, policy revision or ACL;
- candidate/validated/governed state, promotion, publication, supersession, rollback or forgetting;
- rule mutes, Wilson thresholds, scoring offsets or calibration eligibility;
- whether human review is required or whether a reviewer is authorized; or
- reminders, delivery, external execution or Expert Brain changes.

Those decisions are made by typed facts, integer arithmetic, database authority, current tenant
policy and explicit human action. Model prose cannot be treated as evidence of approval and cannot
invoke a publisher directly. If a model is unavailable or uncertain, the system holds/rejects the
input; it does not silently substitute model judgment for deterministic authority.

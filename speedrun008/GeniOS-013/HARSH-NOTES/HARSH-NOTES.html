01The story in one page

Read this section first. Everything after it is the evidence.

  • The test. On 23 Oct Claude read Rohit's Gmail and wrote an ideal dashboard: 20 cards and 10 goals, every card tied to a goal. GeniOS's own first real week (22–28 Oct) made 111 cards, none on a goal, and 11 goals that stayed "proposed". Card by card: 0 the same, 9 partly or wrong, 11 missing.
  • The model is not the bottleneck. Given a clean piece of work, the L2 judge read the accelerator case (i1, the launch film's own example) almost exactly like the ideal. The claim checker then held the answer, and the founder saw "We held the one we found…". Meanwhile an old rule card said "you are 71 days late", which was false.
  • What broke: mail grouped by sender instead of by piece of work; the judge woken only by new mail, never by silence; approved knowledge that never reached the model; good answers held by the checker; nothing the brain concluded saved; six different card makers.
  • The root. Atlas v2's L3 has two halves: the Evidence Graph (what sources said) and the Intelligence Graph (what GeniOS concluded). Only the first was built.
  • The fix. Keep one small saved page per piece of work. Wake the brain when something changed or should have happened. The brain reads the page plus only what is new; code checks the update and saves it; cards are views of pages and name their goal. Five parts, four loops.
  • Proved locally first. A local copy of the org and a notebook prototype (b2) found the work for all 17 visible ideal cards for $8.41, and showed the next failures. The plan fixes those too.

02The test GeniOS must pass first: i1

From the launch film: GeniOS is "a 24/7 consultant for our company, which says: do this instead of that, to make this happen and get that projected goal", with approve-before-send and "every Done teaches".

What happened

An accelerator rejected the application. The founder asked them to reconsider; they said "resend it and keep us posted". Three follow-ups went out in 20 days with no reply. Then priorities changed and the founder forgot.

What GeniOS must say

Goal: get the accelerator to reconsider · at risk · found in your mail. "Send one update with a new, checkable fact, then stop until they answer." The door is open in writing and three updates got no reply, so the next must carry a new fact: that day's incubator milestone.

04Why the cards kept failing, stage by stage

Follow one mail from capture to card. Every number is measured on the local copy of the first week.

Input (L1): mostly fine, with holes

  • 1,829 mails from 25 Apr to 28 Oct: 1,193 emitted and 636 archived. The 547 "parked" rows are attachment stubs whose mails were read.
  • Archived mail carries no text by design (F37), and some of it mattered: f2's "still incomplete" reminders.
  • Only 180 days are captured, so i6, s1, s2 and one ideal goal don't exist for GeniOS.
  • The 11 Aug investor update was labelled contract_renewal ten times. 80 traction amounts sit as facts on the founder's own person node and are never compared. 7 bounces join no work.

Grouping: the wrong unit of work

A "file" is one sender's correlations, recomputed on every read (264 files). One application sits in 2 files and the incubator's programme in 4. The intro platform is a single file of 500 mails, 322 threads and 61 people, refused 31 times with 0 tokens, so it was never judged. That loses f3, b1 and b3.

Waking: only new mail, on a UTC clock

Knowledge: approved, but never shown to the model

  • The Atlas's Domain Compiler is built and made 100 packages in the week, but only the template lane reads them. reason/l2 and reason/expert import none of it: the lane with the knowledge has no model, and the lane with the model has no knowledge.
  • Knowledge is chosen only through an accepted brief line, and 0 of 29 were accepted, so every dossier said "No playbook for this file".
  • The reader needs exactly one staged playbook; 2 of 238 have stages, so Admin's approved renewal and follow-up knowledge is invisible. 165 units were approved and never switched to stable.

Judgment (L2): good reasoning, thrown away

Card making: six writers, no rule for "a person is needed"

  • The machine's words reach the person: "We held the one we found…" on 13 open cards, 40 headlines starting "Needs a decision —", internal keys such as dependency_stated, ": undefined" in the Mac app.
  • Wrong urgency: severity is a utility score cut at 70 and 85, so 22 of 103 open cards are critical, while every question card scores 0 and is never shown first.
  • Nothing closes: every open card expires within a week (the card reuses a 168-hour decision window), Done is only a click, and 0 cards closed on proof.
  • Built but unwired: delegation, the outbox (0 rows), the card writer R-10 (off), and a shared card reader most screens don't use.

05Why goals never came

1 · One door, never opened

A goal is live only when its brief line is accepted. 0 of 29 were. Cards show approved goals only, and proposed goals may not be used in reasoning.

2 · Proposed from the wrong unit

The weekly drafter reads the 40 latest thread objectives; L2 proposes at most one goal per sender file. That is how a government portal became an investor.

3 · A goal is only a sentence

No target, plan, progress, status or next move. It can't say what is missing, so no card can come from a goal (f3, i1).

4 · Nobody judges a goal

No "at risk", no "what's next", no gap card. In the notebook the grain was wrong too: per-investor goals, a 163-situation activity goal, two link sets disagreeing on 53 of 233.

The plan: P2 builds a real goal object (target with its quote, plan, progress counted from plan rows, status, next move, parent). Goals may be used, labelled, while unconfirmed, and the founder confirms with one click. P4 judges goals; P5 raises gap, reached and pattern cards from them, and every card names its goal.

06Where the business situation broke

  • Formed by rules, not reasoning: 1,528 situations from a fixed list of types, driving 71 of 103 cards.
  • Quiet work goes to sleep after 45 days (282 dormant), exactly where i2 and b3 live.
  • Corrections aren't saved: L2 said the portal thread "is a government portal correction, not an investor relationship"; it is still an investor relationship.
  • Notes never close: 87 of 87 promises are open (83 past due). On i1, "promise open" was false (updates went on 11, 13 and 26 Aug), and the rule engine turned it into "71 days late".
  • Facts don't travel between situations, and founder acts, executions and Screen are stored but never joined to the work. The founder was asked 40 questions; 0 answers came back.
  • Evidence text expires 180 days after capture, not after the work ends. The database is over its ceiling (570 MB against 472 MB).

10The fix: five parts and four loops

Each part folder in GeniOS-013/ has 01 what exists, 02 why it fails, 03 expected vs happening, 04 the steps with tests and done-when, 05 production, 06 a new customer, 07 the full spec and 08 the requirements trace.

P1

Orchestrator

What runs, when, for which situation or goal, in what order, at what cost. The company clock, nine named triggers, the date index, spend in priority order, the run ledger, switches and the parity gate, the worker and queue, the replay.

Never judges, stores or words a card; makes no model call.

P2

Notebook (L3)

The evidence and the conclusions: company page, parties, situation pages, goals, loops, claims, links, founder acts. Code admission before anything is kept; lifecycle; retention that follows the work.

Never decides what to conclude.

P3

Plane D

What a professional knows: playbooks, rules with their conditions, stages, durations. Reuse the built compiler and join it to the model; review Founder Office; one act approves and switches on.

Never reasons.

P4

Plane R

How a professional thinks: the model as expert, a library of reasoning units, calculators, precedence, printed ids only, goals judged as goals, one judge per tenant at the parity gate.

Never stores or renders.

P5

Cards

One card per situation, raised only when reasoning says a person is needed. Every card names its goal, closes on proof, speaks to "you", shows no held text, and reads the same on every screen.

Never judges the situation behind it.

Ten rules every step follows

  1. One source of truth per rule, in Plane D or R, never in prompt text.
  2. The model judges; code remembers, calculates and checks.
  3. Nothing is kept unless backed; a refused change is held with its reason.
  4. Unknown is not zero.
  5. Time is local: the company zone and UTC on every instant.
  6. Prove it in the notebook first, then port.
  7. Measure before and after on the acceptance set, and log it.
  8. Attribute every failure to a part before fixing it.
  9. One writer per file; tests, measurement and Rohit's look before done.
  10. Fixed words: notebook, situation, goal, card, Plane D, Plane R, orchestrator, loop.

11Decisions settled while checking the plan, 30 Oct

Checking P1 against 479 requirements found cross-part disagreements. These are now binding in the documents.

TopicDecision
RetriesOne input gets at most one extra paid call across the repair, the generic retry and the timeout retry together, so never a third payment (P4 07 §15). P1 adds no attempt; it parks the subject.
TransactionsOne per sweep with a savepoint per subject. A crash loses only the open sweep's writes, never answers already received.
One due per cardcard_due follows P5's by: the act-by for a decision, the next check for a waiting card. Reminders never become the due.
Reason codesOne closed list in P1 07 §10.1 (domain_off, zone_unconfirmed, not_due …). A code outside it fails guard OG-8.
Daily cap$0.50 default for a founder-size tenant; it changes only by a decision in 08-LOG. Severity-4 items pass it.
ConcurrencyOne Evolve run and one Act run per tenant at a time, never two on one subject. 1 worker until the load drill passes, then 4.
Founder defaultsFounder Office and Admin live; Sales and Support off (shadow only for a company that sells or supports).
New tenantsMove to the new path only after the golden tenant and two more design partners pass the acceptance set.
ReopenA resolved situation reopens only within 90 days; after that the evidence is new work.
f2 on 24 OctVerify first ("No confirmation by that day"); missed only on the person's word.
Light runsA supplied document, a met evidence need and "look again" each get one delta call; "look again" runs even when nothing is new.

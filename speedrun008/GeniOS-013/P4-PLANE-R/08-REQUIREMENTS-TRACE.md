# P4 · Plane R · 08 · Every requirement, and where it is built

**What this file is:** the trace from each requirement the five sources place on Plane R to the section of [07](07-THE-PLANE-R-SPEC.md) that specifies it and the step of [04](04-WHAT-NEEDS-TO-BE-DONE-NOW.md) that builds it. The requirements were extracted on 30 Oct from Design Atlas v2 (with the GeniOS-008 architecture), the GeniOS-008 L2 plan and Rohit's own L2 spec, the expected outcomes, the ideal dashboard and the launch film (with Rohit's description of the loops and Rohit's own words on how the expert thinks), then checked one by one against the rewritten P4 by independent checkers.

**No names:** cases go by their ideal ids (`p1`, `f1` …), as in [10](../10-ACCEPTANCE.md).

**Key:** ✅ specified and built by a step · 🟡 partly · ❌ missing · ↗ another part's job.

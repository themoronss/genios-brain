# P4 · Plane R · 04 · What needs to be done now

**The full specification is [07](07-THE-PLANE-R-SPEC.md).** This file holds the decisions and the step-by-step build. Every step cites the section of 07 it builds. [08](08-REQUIREMENTS-TRACE.md) shows that each requirement from the Atlas v2, the GeniOS-008 L2 plan with Rohit's L2 spec, the ideal dashboard, the expected outcomes and the film lands in a step.

**The principle is Rohit's own:** the model is the expert; code is memory, calculator and checker; patterns, not rigid rules. So:
1. write the ways of thinking down once, as units, and generate every prompt from them;
2. give the model the numbers and dates it must not guess, in the company's own time;
3. check what it claims, compute what it must not assert (confidence, the lane), and keep what stands;
4. reason about goals and across situations, not only one thread at a time;
5. end with one judge in the product.

The L2 judge built on 18 Oct is the base. Rewrite nothing that is right: its gate, its claim check, its store and its needs stay.

Every step is proved in the notebook first. A step is done only when:
- its tests pass;
- its done-when holds;
- the guards it owns (07 §20) pass;
- the acceptance set ([10](../10-ACCEPTANCE.md)) shows nothing worse, including must-apply recall at least 90%, must-abstain at least 95% and forbidden outputs 0 (07 §19).

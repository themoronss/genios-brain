## The notebook's card content already matches the ideal

On the cards it raised, it already writes the ideal's kind of content: verb-first headlines, a why-now line, a recommendation, at most 4 steps, verbatim quotes, and a draft where a message is due. Examples, without names:
- "Submit the application before it closes tomorrow" (`f2`, same as the ideal);

- "Confirm the candidate's status and prepare for the promised paid transition" (`h1`, same).

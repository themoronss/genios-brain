# P5 · Cards · 08 · Every requirement, and where it is built

**What this file is:** the trace from each requirement the six sources place on the cards part to the section of [07](07-THE-CARDS-SPEC.md) that specifies it and the step of [04](04-WHAT-NEEDS-TO-BE-DONE-NOW.md) that builds it. The requirements were extracted on 30 Oct from the ideal dashboard, Design Atlas v2 (with the GeniOS-008 architecture), Rohit's card work (GeniOS-009 to GeniOS-012, with Rohit's review rounds), the expected outcomes, the launch film and the other four parts of GeniOS-013, then checked one by one against the new P5 by independent checkers.

**No names:** cases go by their ideal ids (`p1`, `f1` …), as in [10](../10-ACCEPTANCE.md).

**Key:** ✅ specified and built by a step · 🟡 partly · ❌ missing · ↗ another part's job.

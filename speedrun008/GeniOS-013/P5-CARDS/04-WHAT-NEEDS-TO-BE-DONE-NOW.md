# P5 · Cards · 04 · What needs to be done now

**The full specification is [07](07-THE-CARDS-SPEC.md).** This file holds the decisions and the step-by-step build. Every step cites the section of 07 it builds. [08](08-REQUIREMENTS-TRACE.md) shows that each requirement from the ideal dashboard, the Atlas v2, Rohit's card work (GeniOS-009 to GeniOS-012), the expected outcomes, the film and the other four parts lands in a step.

**The principle is the one P4 already holds:** the model is the expert; code is memory, calculator and checker. For cards that means:
1. a card shows what the reasoning concluded, and never judges again on its own;
2. one situation is one card, versioned, and the number of cards falls by merging and by the raise rule, never by hiding;
3. every sentence on a card has its evidence, in the person's own words, and the checker's words never show;
4. a card names its goal, its kind, its deadline and how we will know it is done, and it closes on proof;
5. what the person does with it comes back, and nothing leaves on their behalf without their approval of the exact words;
6. the same card on every surface, through one reader.

**Much is built already; rewrite nothing that is right:** the living card with its versions (STEP-14), the action door and its refusals, the delegation ledger with frozen bytes, the card writer R-10 (off), the timing gate and the outbox (never used on a delivery), the rebuilt Cards page on `origin/main`, and the approved Mac design on its branch.

Every step is proved in the notebook first, and every screen change follows the dummy-first rule (07 §12.13). A step is done only when:
- its tests pass;
- its done-when holds;
- the guards it owns (07 §19) pass;
- the acceptance set ([10](../10-ACCEPTANCE.md)) shows nothing worse than the last run (07 §16.5).

**The targets, and the step from which each must hold:** at most 30 open cards with no ideal card lost and 0 duplicates from P5-1, and 0 held text from P5-7 (no step before it adds a held-text card); `b3` waiting with its auto-close from P5-1; `i1` and `f3` matching the ideal from P5-2; a goal's next move equal to the ideal's on at least 7 of 9 from P5-3; the rank test (2 critical, 6 high, 12 standard, `p1` the banner) from P5-5; at least 15 of 17 in-copy ideal cards same or close and 0 opposite calls from P5-7, and on every step after. Before the step that owns a target, a step may not make that target worse.

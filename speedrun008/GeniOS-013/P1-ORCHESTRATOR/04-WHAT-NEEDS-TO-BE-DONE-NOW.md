# P1 · Orchestrator · 04 · What needs to be done now

**The full specification is [07](07-THE-ORCHESTRATOR-SPEC.md).** This file holds the decisions and the step-by-step build. Every step cites the section of 07 it builds. [08](08-REQUIREMENTS-TRACE.md) shows that each requirement from Design Atlas v2, the ideal dashboard, the expected outcomes, the earlier plans, the film and the other four parts lands in a step.

**The principle is the rest of the plan's:** the model is the expert; code is memory, calculator and checker. For the Orchestrator that means:
1. P1 never judges, stores, authors or words anything, and makes no model call of its own; it decides what runs, when, for which subject, in what order, at what cost, and records it;
2. nothing runs without a named trigger, every wake-up fires once, and a date wakes a judgment, never a card;
3. the company's clock everywhere, and no run reads the wall clock;
4. work in proportion to what changed: Evolve touches only its subjects, whole-company work only in Maintain;
5. one cap, held in the database, spent in priority order, never silently short;
6. one row per run, and every switch checked against what ran.

There are two tracks:
- **The notebook track (P1-1 to P1-11).** It builds the four loops locally, on the copy, and proves them. Two steps in product code follow it: the worker and the queue (P1-12), and the load drill on the local copy (P1-13). [05](05-HOW-AT-PRODUCTION-LEVEL.md) says how the same thing goes into the product afterwards.
- **The relief track (R1 to R9).** Small changes to today's product. They can start now, in parallel, each behind a row, so the product stops filling its day with one tenant and a new design partner can be onboarded safely. They need Rohit's approval and Harsh's hands. Each production read they need runs on Rohit's yes for that read, through the staff read route, with its output kept out of the repo (07 §11.7).

**Much is built already; rewrite nothing that is right:** capture's per-source cadence (Gmail every 15.5 min), the durable sync jobs that checkpoint and resume, single flight per org (`org_run_leases`), the warm lane's queue pattern (`SKIP LOCKED`, leases, back-off, park after 5), switches as rows per tenant, daily health per tenant, and the notebook's as-of discipline and its one model gateway.

A step is done only when:
- its tests pass;
- its done-when holds;
- the guards it owns (07 §19) pass, and the repo-wide CI guards stay green, the card guard, the situation-site ratchet and the layer guard among them (07 §14.5);
- the acceptance set ([10](../10-ACCEPTANCE.md)) shows nothing worse than the last run (07 §14.5);
- for a step that reaches the product (P1-11, P1-12, each R-step): it was deployed alone, and its one-day number on the daily health row was read and written in 08-LOG before the next deploy; the release gate (07 §11.7, OG-20) holds the next deploy until then.

**How the steps are built:** new code beside the old path, measured on the golden board and on the design partner's copy before any tenant's switch; one writer per file at a time; guards run per group of 2 or 3 coupled steps, the full suites in CI; a test creates only the scratch databases it needs, and drops them.

**The targets, and the step from which each must hold:** every run with its ledger row from P1-2; no run ever short without saying so from P1-7; a quiet day at 0 model calls, an Evolve day under $0.50 and each rung fired once from P1-4; a fresh onboarding build passing G-Onboard (at least 11 of 17 same or close, 0 opposite, `f1`, `f2` and `p1` in wave 1, under $10) from P1-3; every replay event on its day, the clock-only events to 2 Nov included, from P1-8; the onboarding drill's targets from P1-10; no new tenant needing a switch flipped by hand, and 10 §5's end-state targets (at least 15 of 17 same or close, 0 opposite) read before any tenant's switch, from P1-11; the freshness promises (07 §15.2) at 2, 5 and 10 tenants from P1-13. Before the step that owns a target, a step may not make that target worse.

### P1-1 · One clock
- **What** (07 §2):
  - a new module, `nb/clock.py`, taking the company's zone (from the company page; `orgs.timezone` in the copy), a seat's own zone when its seat record sets one (07 §2.1), and the build's instant, with `stamp(t)`, `day(t)`, `weekday(d)`, `relative(t, now)` (computed at view time, never stored; "this week", "next week", "(rolling)" and "anytime" read by `week(t)`; a past instant that is not a due, such as a source's `last_read`, reads "N min ago", "N h ago", then the date), `rung(due, now)`, `day_end(d)`, `week(t)`, `period(t, kind)`, `period_end(kind, t)`, `working_days(a, b)`, `next_working_morning(t)` and `local_hour(tenant, name)` (07 §2.2);
  - the company page gains the zone's confirmed flag, the working week, the holidays, `week_start` (Monday by default) and `fiscal_year_start` (1 Jan by default) (P2 §3.4, §20.8; 07 §2.4); each rung kind's non-working rule is a data row `clock.py` reads, and the unit (calendar or working days) of a lead time, a stage norm or a ladder day is a field on its source row, never code (07 §2.4, §4.1);
  - used by `judge.py` (the system prompt's "now", the dossier header, timeline lines, meetings), `checks.py` and `cards.py` (their "today", "passed" and the 7-day window), the date index (P1-4) and the caps (P1-7); no other module turns an instant into local time (OG-3);
  - `eval_time` passed into every call of a run and stored on its row; no run-path module reads the wall clock (OG-2); every saved version, card version and ledger row carries the evidence time (the run's `eval_time`) and the recorded time (the wall clock at write) (07 §2.6); no P1 write stores a value derived from now, such as an age or days waiting (OG-3).
- **Guards:** OG-2, OG-3.
- **Test** (`tests/test_clock.py`; 07 §18, Clock):

  - a date with no time is a whole local day: `day_end(24 Oct)` in India is 24 Oct, 18:29:59 UTC, so a loop due 24 Oct is not broken at 24 Oct 00:00 UTC or at 18:29 UTC, and is broken from 25 Oct 00:00 IST; it never passes at 23:59:59 UTC; a rung changes at local midnight, not UTC midnight;
  - a `window_end` due of Sun 25 Oct reads "this week" on Fri 23 Oct and "passed" on Mon 26 Oct; a dated due of Fri 23 Oct reads "later today" on 23 Oct; a rolling `window_end` recomputes its end at each local midnight and keeps reading "this week (rolling)"; a due with no date reads "anytime" every day; a `last_read` 4 minutes back reads "4 min ago", 70 minutes back "1 h ago", and neither phrase is stored;
  - `week(t)` on Fri 23 Oct 2026 gives W43, 19 to 25 Oct, and an activity tile reads that range; with `week_start` Sunday it gives "week of 18 Oct", 18 to 24 Oct; a week before connection reads "not measured", never 0;
  - on 23 Oct 2026 the company month reads day 23 of 31 and the pace is round(23/31) = 74%, computed from the clock, never typed; with a calendar fiscal year this quarter ends 31 Dec at local midnight, and with a 1 Apr `fiscal_year_start` it ends 31 Dec too; a month end in a leap February is the 29th;
  - a zone with summer time is right on both sides of the change;
  - 1 Nov 2026 prints as a Sunday; a typed weekday that disagrees with its date is never printed;
  - a two-working-day reply owed from a Friday is due on Tuesday; a follow-up after a Friday meeting is due on Monday; the same 2-day lead time over a weekend gives two due dates when its unit field is calendar and when it is working, and changing the field changes the date with no code change;
  - with the zone unconfirmed, every instant prints UTC with "time zone unconfirmed" and nothing says "today";
  - a seat with its own zone gets its 08:00 and its local day from `local_hour` in that zone, while rungs, caps and "today" on a card stay on the company's zone; both zones print with UTC beside them;
  - every saved version, card version and `loop_runs` row has both times; in a replay the evidence time is the replayed instant and the recorded time the wall clock; a version Onboard makes from history carries the run's `eval_time`, and its history line is dated by the newest cited evidence's `occurred_at`, so a version citing 6 Aug mail lists before one citing 7 Aug, whatever order they were written in;
  - a seeded `datetime.now()` on the run path fails OG-2; a seeded Maintain job that writes `days_waiting` fails OG-3, and the same figure computed through `clock.relative` at view time passes.

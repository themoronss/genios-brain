# P3 · Plane D · 03 · What's expected, and what's happening

**Expected**, from Rohit's own words:
- **the film:** the engine combines "domain expertise" with reasoning patterns and the company's state, for "every department, every departmental goal, every situation";
- **the Founder Office question:** "How would a seasoned chief of staff to a founder read this situation?";
- **the design rule:** "reviewed by that founder line by line" (`06` D45);
- **the ideal dashboard:** it cites Plane D's own rules by name ("investors do not chase", "silence reads by stage", "only an offer counts", "a pass is a date, not a verdict"). Plane D is the knowledge the ideal was written from.

| # | Expected | Happening |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Every situation is read with the knowledge that applies to it** | 29% of the notebook's situations had expertise behind them; 71% had none |
| 2 | **All the knowledge that applies, from every department.** A renewal is a renewal, whichever domain wrote it | One key (kind of work), one domain. Admin's approved renewal and follow-up knowledge never reaches the model |
| 3 | **A rule arrives with its conditions,** so it's used where it applies and not where it doesn't | Statements and "why" only. "It reads their quiet, never ours" was lost, and the model used it on our own silence |
| 4 | **The whole playbook, with its moves in full** | Product: all steps. Notebook: only the first step of each move |
| 5 | **Reviewed by the founder, line by line** | Founder Office: draft, unreviewed, written by AI. 22 draft situations corpus-wide; their cards are demoted to observations |
| 6 | **Every department** (the film: Admin, Sales, Finance, Support, Projects, Hiring) | Admin, Sales and Support are deep but generic; Founder Office is deep in 2 of 6 kinds. Finance exists only as Admin's "finance administration" group. Projects has no domain. Hiring is a Founder Office stub |
| 7 | **Knowledge that is right** | Mostly right and sourced. Two older Admin rules made wrong cards (a gate too wide, a quote dropped) |
| 8 | **Measured: each piece of knowledge proves itself** | No test case ties a rule to the cards it should or shouldn't produce |

## Where the knowledge is already strong

The investor and programme playbooks and their ten rules of thumb are the knowledge the ideal dashboard used:
- the stages with typical durations, each a labelled prior with its source;
- what quiet means at each stage;
- the moves step by step;
- the conditions and failure modes of each rule.

They need to arrive whole and be reviewed, not rewritten.

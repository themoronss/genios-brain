# Golden labels — should this have reached you?

**Written for:** Rohit. **What it is:** the answer key of the founder golden set (`STEP-01` §3.5,
decision `D12`). Ten minutes of your time; every later step is judged against it.

**How to answer.** One question per row: *should this have reached you?* Write one of three answers
in **Your answer**, and change **Labelled by** to `rohit`:

| Answer | Means |
|---|---|
| `yes` | a card, on the day it matters |
| `brief only` | a line in the morning brief, not a card |
| `no` | archived — kept, never surfaced |

Until you answer a row, **my answer counts, and the row says so** (`D12a` default). Each item is a
sender and a date only — no subject, no text (`D12b`). The cases built from these rows are
synthetic: invented names and text, the same shape (`tests/replays/specs/founder/`, `D12e`).

**How a row is scored** (`D12d`): `yes` → the case must produce the right card, and nothing
forbidden. `brief only` → not expressible until `STEP-15` builds the brief, and counted apart.
`no` → the case must produce no card — and must show it reached the stage where that was decided,
or it counts as *not exercised*, never as a pass.

## Why I answered as I did

- **`yes`** where somebody waits on you, a deadline or a meeting is coming, or an application you
  made moved — 1–7, 9–13, 15–17, 19, 24–29.
- **`brief only`** where the right behaviour is to know, not to act today: introductions nobody
  answered (8), an investor who asked for news only when there is real news (14), programs that
  wrote without asking anything (18, 20–23), and your own WhatsApp promises, which belong in the
  files they are about (30).
- **`no`** for 31–40: each is a shape GeniOS has already got wrong on your account (`04` §2,
  `STEP-01` §4).

A `brief only` answer is scored as *not expressible* until `STEP-15` builds the morning brief —
the board counts it apart rather than as a pass or a fail.

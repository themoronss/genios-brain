## Blunt operational reading

Layer 3 has a serious deterministic compiler, typed package and deterministic four-brain snapshot resolution. Those are verified successes. It does **not** currently provide deep, authoritative expertise to the shown cards: the compiler is default-off and shadow-only, the active reasoning path still consumes `SALES_V1`/`GENERAL_V1`, most corpus files are stubs, and every non-stub capability remains an unreviewed draft. The right diagnosis is not “the new files are present but only need wiring.” Reachability, expert authorship, review-state admission, downstream semantic consumption and customer authority are all incomplete.

At the current Layer 3→4 adapter, **hash-only influence is provenance, not semantic influence**: Organization, Behavior and Adaptive values change `knowledge_hash`, but no typed projection proves they change a constraint, candidate, rank or wording. Corpus vector order below is `total/stub/non-stub/reviewed/accepted`, followed by Admin routes: **Sales 46/43/3/0/0; Support 49/40/9/0/0; Admin 57/57/0/0/0 routes**. **Improvement and acceptance evidence are separate columns** because a proposed repair is not proof that it works.

## What verified success does and does not mean

The compiler’s deterministic construction, snapshot hashing, visibility filtering and two-axis conflict policy are real engineering assets. The corpus validator’s **0 errors** means the checked artifacts satisfy structural invariants. It does not mean all professional knowledge is deep or accepted: the same run emits **715 warnings**, and all 12 non-stub authored drafts across Sales and Support are `status: draft`, `review_status: unreviewed`, with no reviewer. Likewise, a shadow decision proves comparability and safety instrumentation; it does not prove the customer received the decision.

The legacy packs are also functioning software. `packs/wiring.py:9-23` registers `SALES_V1` and `GENERAL_V1`, and `reason/runner.py:525-540` continues through the effective legacy pack after an optional shadow pass. That explains why cards are consistently produced. The failure is that consistency is mistaken for expert judgment: active templates reduce very different relationships to “reply,” “confirm,” “send recap,” or “reset the plan.”

## Required expected behavior

For a supported situation, Layer 3 should return the exact accepted capability versions, reviewer and acceptance receipt, required professional objects, applicable rules, candidate playbooks, known failure patterns, four-brain selections/exclusions, source receipts, coverage and missing inputs. For an unsupported, all-stub or draft/unreviewed situation, it should return an explicit uncovered/unaccepted result with no action-authorizing playbook. It must never paper over missing Layer 1 identity or Layer 2 state by choosing a generic capability.

The customer-visible success gate is therefore traceable authority: the final card must identify the package and snapshot that constrained reasoning; golden scenarios must demonstrate that malformed or uncovered inputs abstain; and a completed action must later reconcile against an observable outcome. Until those receipts exist, Layer 3 remains **framework-ready, not live-ready**, with bounded verified successes and material current failures.

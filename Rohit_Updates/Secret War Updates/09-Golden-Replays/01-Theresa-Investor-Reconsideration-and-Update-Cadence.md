# Golden Replay 01 — Theresa Investor Reconsideration and Update Cadence

## Evidence boundary

This is an HKS acceptance replay, not a claim about a live Theresa record. **[CUSTOMER]** states that Theresa, an Antler Singapore partner, asked Rohit to keep sending updates and said she could reconsider; Rohit later sent two or three updates without a reply. No supplied screenshot visibly proves that exact thread, wording, number of updates, current consent, or present relationship state. The screenshots prove a broader surface symptom only: cards can reduce long histories to generic “reply now,” show scalar confidence, and infer an imperative from message age.

**[CODE]** establishes risk, not the cause of a particular screenshot: Layer 1 lacks a mandatory typed business-role boundary; Layer 2 can emit semantically thin context; Layer 3 has no accepted investor/fundraising lane, while its deep compiler is default-off/shadow and legacy Sales can remain authoritative; Layer 4 projection can lose stakes and Completion; card acceptance is not welded to native execution; outcome learning is fragmented. Therefore this replay defines what must be deterministically proven before GeniOS can advise on Theresa.

## Current failure versus expected behavior

**Current failure:** the reported product interpretation is “you were rejected; one last chance,” while the actual customer need is conditional cadence intelligence: determine whether anything materially new exists, whether Theresa's permission remains valid, whether prior updates already satisfied the ask, and whether the next best move is send, wait, connector nudge, or stop. The failure fabricates stage and scarcity, turns silence into urgency, and makes the founder reconstruct the relationship.

**Expected behavior:** with complete evidence and accepted expertise, the card says which new milestone qualifies, ties it to Theresa's original request, names the exact update content and recipient, compares waiting, states Rohit's approval boundary, and defines delivery and response windows. Without a new material milestone, the correct decision is **No action / stop until trigger**, not a lower-confidence follow-up.

## Prohibited behavior

- Do not state “rejected,” “last chance,” active opportunity stage, deadline, consent, or likely reconsideration without evidence.
- Do not equate no reply with deal cooling, urgency, or permission to send repeatedly.
- Do not use a connector as the action target or merge separate investor relationships.
- Do not allow a generic Sales pack or fluent LLM explanation to launder missing fundraising expertise.
- Do not label card display, “I'll do it,” draft, send, delivery, or open as reconsideration or revenue value.
- Do not learn a global cadence rule from Theresa's single thread or from silence alone.

## Outcome and exit gate

The replay is green only if every mutation produces the declared class and all actionable variants carry Business subject, roles, exact `what remains`, material trigger, evidence/freshness/conflicts, accepted expertise/four-brain coverage, primary decision, real Alternative, stop rule, owner/approval, Completion and Outcome window. Critical metrics are zero fabricated stage/urgency, zero wrong-target/connector sends, zero send without material trigger, zero prescription when expertise is uncovered, and zero duplicate cross-channel update.

Customer value is evaluated later: did GeniOS surface a genuinely material update the founder would otherwise have missed, reduce reconstruction/correction effort, and contribute to a reply or reconsideration inside the predeclared window? Until actual action, external result, counterfactual and alternative causes reconcile, the only proven result is safe decision behavior—not ROI.

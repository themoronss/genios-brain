# Golden Replay 11 — Revenue Impact, Missed Opportunity, and Counterfactual Proof

## Evidence status and scenario boundary

This is a **[MODELLED]** economic-proof replay derived from the customer value contract and application reference. The **[CUSTOMER]** requirement is truthful proof that GeniOS helped produce a material result; the fixture itself is not customer testimony or a production win. **[CODE]** refers to `harsh/mvp@b739bd5`; **[ATLAS]** is intended architecture. The assertions become **[TEST]** evidence only after execution. No amount or Outcome below is claimed as live revenue.

## Business subject and base fixture

The **Business subject** is one exact opportunity and its relationship-scoped unresolved decision—not a contact node, account-wide activity stream, card, delivery, or later revenue row.

At `t0`, opportunity `opp-42` has a verified current state, named counterparty and a time-bounded open loop. Before GeniOS surfaces anything, the fixture records the founder's otherwise-action: no task exists, no equivalent message is scheduled, and the founder reports they would review this account only in the normal weekly pipeline pass. GeniOS then produces a versioned decision with a primary action, materially distinct Alternative, wait/stop option, expected consequence, expiry, Completion predicate, outcome window and attribution plan.

The replay records each later boundary independently: recommendation exposure; accepted/modified/rejected claim; actual execution; provider acceptance/delivery; counterparty response; declared Completion; opportunity-stage or cash result; competing causes; and the counterfactual/control observation. A 2–4 week design-partner result window and 60–90 day economic window are evaluation hypotheses from the contract, not guaranteed causal horizons.

## Current failure

**[CODE]** contains structured outcomes, deterministic learning units and useful execution/delivery primitives, but the canonical vertical identity is fragmented. Card acceptance is not atomically welded to ExecutionObject progress; legacy/v2 delivery truth differs; client receipts can overstate execution; the value API is hardcoded to zero even while outcome paths exist; and no joined recommendation→exposure→action→delivery→external result→counterfactual ledger is proved.

This creates two opposite failures. The product may under-report real value as zero, or it may overclaim a displayed card, click, send or temporally later deal movement as revenue impact. Hindsight can also turn any positive result into “would have missed,” while negative, never-delivered and founder-modified cases disappear from the denominator. Neither is credible economic proof.

## Expected behavior

The decision and otherwise-action are pre-registered before exposure. Every transition has one canonical event identity with source aliases, version and timestamp. Missing stages remain unknown; they are never inferred from an earlier stage. A counterparty reply or stage change qualifies only when it matches the exact opportunity, action, relationship and declared window. Delivery, Completion, business outcome and revenue are separate.

Attribution is conservative and explicit:

- **caused** requires a declared counterfactual/control design and evidence that the outcome would probably not have occurred without the intervention;
- **assisted** means GeniOS materially changed discovery, timing, content or execution, but another route might have produced the action/outcome;
- **associated** means exposure preceded the result without enough causal evidence;
- **unknown** means action, result, window, otherwise-action or competing-cause evidence is incomplete;
- **neutral/cancelled-by-world** covers acquisition, role change or external events that make efficacy unevaluable.

“Would-have-missed” is a pre-registered candidate class. The absence of a prior task plus delayed baseline review can support assisted/causal evaluation, but a good outcome alone never proves it. Comparable negative and no-outcome cases remain in the eligible cohort. Revenue is counted only from an authoritative finance/CRM receipt and is not attributed merely because an opportunity advanced.

## Prohibited behavior

- Do not call card display, view, click, “I'll do it,” send, delivered/opened or meeting booked automatic revenue value.
- Do not infer execution from acceptance, Completion from delivery, or business outcome from Completion.
- Do not create the counterfactual after observing the result.
- Do not classify “would have missed” because the card was old, high-score or followed by a win.
- Do not join outcomes person-wide, account-wide or outside the declared opportunity/window.
- Do not exclude rejected, modified, failed-delivery, negative, neutral or unknown cases from the denominator.
- Do not treat temporal order, model confidence or salesperson belief as causality.
- Do not sum associated/unknown value into attributed ROI or convert a modelled target into Live proof.

## Attribution ledger contract

| Ledger stage | Required proof | Must not imply |
|---|---|---|
| Eligible decision | Current BSO, accepted expertise, frozen decision and evaluation plan before exposure | Correct action or value |
| Exposure | Authenticated impression of exact decision/version | User read, accepted or acted |
| Claim | Accepted, modified or rejected response by exact actor | Execution or Completion |
| Execution | External/internal action receipt tied to action ID | Provider delivery or business outcome |
| Delivery | Definite provider result or explicit unknown | Counterparty engagement or success |
| Completion | Declared real-world loop resolution | Opportunity/revenue impact automatically |
| Outcome | Scoped external business result inside window | GeniOS causality |
| Counterfactual | Pre-registered baseline/control/otherwise-action and competing causes | Certainty; quality and limitations remain visible |
| Attribution | Caused/assisted/associated/unknown/neutral under declared method | Universal product efficacy or market proof |

## Replay assertions

1. The ledger cannot transition to a later stage without that stage's own receipt; no inference from the prior stage is allowed.
2. Otherwise-action, candidates, consequence, Completion, window and attribution method are frozen before outcome evidence becomes available.
3. Duplicate source aliases resolve to one outcome; conflicting identities quarantine rather than attach by person/company.
4. Associated and unknown amounts are excluded from attributed ROI and reported separately.
5. All eligible negative, neutral, never-delivered and modified cases remain visible with reason-coded denominator treatment.
6. API totals exactly equal the canonical ledger by class/window and show data unavailable instead of hardcoded or fabricated numbers.
7. Model-disabled replay yields identical eligibility, joins, outcome labels, attribution class and value arithmetic.

## Outcome

The base replay passes when the exact opportunity chain is complete, the otherwise-action is pre-registered, and the result receives only the attribution class supported by evidence. A genuine “would-have-missed” case may become **assisted** or, under a credible counterfactual/control, **caused**; without that proof it remains associated/unknown. Success is **zero proxy inflation, zero post-hoc counterfactuals, exact ledger/API reconciliation, complete negative-case denominators, and no attributed revenue without authoritative business receipt plus causal method**. Deterministic replay proves accounting logic, not real economic lift; Outcome-proven status requires measured design-partner and longer-window customer evidence.

# Layer 4 — Customer Expectation and HKS

**Evidence baseline:** `harsh/mvp@b739bd5ca682d09550acc400ed2892c38c8518f8`. HKS remains the user’s literal project label; no expansion is invented. Layer 4 consumes a valid Layer 3 `ExpertisePackage` and answers “What should happen?” It must not repair missing identity/context by guessing, execute the action, or call acceptance/completion a business outcome.

## Customer expectation: a decision, not an activity paraphrase

The founder wants GeniOS to remove reconstruction work. A sales consultant would not say merely “reply,” “send recap,” or “reset the plan.” It would identify the unresolved commercial or relationship decision; explain stakes and timing; compare viable moves; recommend the best move for this company and relationship; state the owner, approval boundary and completion signal; and say when waiting or stopping is superior.

| Customer question | Expected reasoning answer | Current-style substitute | Failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| What exactly remains? | One bounded unresolved request, commitment, decision or outcome | “They wrote 30 days ago” | Age becomes intelligence |
| Why does it matter now? | Cost of delay, opportunity, deadline/cadence and expiry | “Urgency 100%” | Scalar has no business stakes |
| What should I do? | Specific action with recipient, content/asset, channel and timing | “Reply now” | User must still think |
| Why this move? | Evidence-linked rationale and rejected alternatives | “Ball in your court” | No comparative judgment |
| What else could work? | Material fallback and its trigger | One imperative | No resilience to non-response |
| Should I wait or stop? | Explicit do-nothing/stop option and consequence | Every silence becomes follow-up | System manufactures busywork |
| Can an agent own it? | Delegation feasibility, required authority and approval | “I’ll do it” button | Acceptance is confused with execution |
| What is done? | Observable external completion condition | Card acknowledged/snoozed | Workflow state is confused with resolution |
| How sure are you? | Evidence, identity, expertise and decision confidence separately | Card score relabelled confidence | Missing expertise is hidden |
| What result matters? | Outcome window and metric | No completion/stakes fields | No learning or ROI proof |

## Decision object the customer should receive

| Field | Expected content | Failure condition |
|---|---|---|
| Situation | State headline, not action headline | “Reply to X” is the situation |
| Remaining loop | Exact open request/commitment/decision | Generic relationship hygiene |
| Primary candidate | Concrete who/what/when/channel/content | Generic verb without object |
| Alternatives | At least one materially distinct fallback where feasible | Rephrased duplicate of primary |
| Do-nothing/stop | When waiting/stopping is best and its cost | Forced action despite weak evidence |
| Stakes and urgency | Business consequence plus expiry/cost-of-delay | Age or score alone |
| Rejections | Candidate, violated constraint and evidence | Hidden elimination |
| Confidence vector | Evidence + identity + expertise + decision calibration | One decorative percentage |
| Owner/approval | Human/agent owner and authority boundary | Auto-delegation without permission |
| Completion/outcome | Observable done event and result window | “I’ll do it” treated as success |

## Golden judgment for the Theresa case

A high-quality result is conditional, not theatrical certainty:

> Theresa asked to receive meaningful progress updates and said she may reconsider. Two or three updates appear sent; no reply is verified. Do not frame this as a new rejection. If there is a material milestone since the last update, send one concise investor update tied to that milestone and the original reconsideration context; otherwise wait until a material update exists. If the agreed cadence has clearly elapsed and a trusted connector is available, consider one permission-safe connector nudge. Stop repetitive updates that add no new information. Completion is delivery to Theresa; the outcome is reply/reconsideration inside the chosen window.

Layer 4 may produce this only if Layer 1–3 supply Theresa’s role, exact request, sent-update evidence, visibility, current company milestone and applicable fundraising expertise. Otherwise the correct result is a specific review/defer request, not this prose as a guess.

## Customer acceptance bar

For every prescriptive HKS result, a reviewer must be able to answer: why this candidate won; what materially distinct candidate lost and why; what waiting/stopping means; which hard constraints eliminated choices; whether an agent can safely own it; what evidence would change the decision; what marks completion; and what business outcome will be observed. If any answer is missing, the reasoning has not reduced founder thinking—it has merely renamed the task.

L2 · why 114 situations can never be admitted

The admission gate has two halves. One is conditional (qes_required, skipped on a tenant whose Layer 1 is not scoring). The other is unconditional on every tenant: no verified evidence span, an open conflict, an unresolved identity, insufficient coverage.

awaiting_response (74) and first_response_overdue (40) are built from a thread’s silence — the finding is that nobody wrote back. There is no sentence to quote, so no verified span can exist, so the unconditional half holds every one of them, for ever. The rule is right for a claim and wrong for an absence. The three types that do pass are all built from something somebody actually wrote.

L2 · the quality bug — the graph knew, the card did not

graph_edges:  sunil.s@sanchiconnect.tech  --owns-->  "provide fundraising opportunities…"

The graph stored the ownership correctly. Then the situation rule builds commitment_overdue for every commitment whose date has passed — without ever reading who owns it:

display_name = f"{name} — promise past due"
facts        = [ ("commitment.owed_to", name, "string"), … ]

…and the card copy hard-codes the voice:

"headline":  "Deliver {action} to {entity} today"
"situation": "{days}d overdue — you promised this"

So a promise the incubator made TO the founder is rendered as a promise the founder owes THEM — imperative voice, critical urgency. Three of the nine cards are this one email. Every layer did what it was told; the ownership fact stops being read one hop before the sentence that needs it.

What to do

The seven losses, and the order to fix them

  1. commitment_overdue must read the owns edge, and the card copy must branch on it. Three of the nine cards are one marketing email rendered as the founder's own overdue promise.
  2. 114 situations that can never be admitted. awaiting_response and first_response_overdue are absence-shaped — the evidence is that nobody replied. Either the gate lets an absence-typed situation cite its anchor's last inbound span, or those types carry a typed-absence receipt.
  3. 159 of 159 attachments unread. The image is built; deploy it, then GENIOS_ENABLE_OCR=true + the org allowlist. The parked files drain themselves.
  4. Three empty brains on 349 packages. Cheapest first: teach the runtime-brain snapshot to union temporary_memories.
  5. Switch sales on for this tenant. Its plays already produce the highest utilities here, and this is a founder's fundraising inbox.
  6. Read the 16 deferrals before touching the floor. A floor nothing trips and a floor everything trips are both wrong; 16 of 33 is neither.
  7. 367 emails dropped at the gate. Correct for newsletters — N-06 is Gmail's own classifier and takes a first-time vendor with it.
  8. 69 events kept at unknown authority. The relevance model was refused because the ambiguous share was over 10% — a graph-coverage symptom, not a relevance one.

What to do

The seven losses, and the order to fix them

  1. 114 situations that can never be admitted. awaiting_response and first_response_overdue are absence-shaped — the evidence is that nobody replied. Either the gate lets an absence-typed situation cite its anchor's last inbound span, or those types carry a typed-absence receipt.
  2. 159 of 159 attachments unread. The image is built; deploy it, then GENIOS_ENABLE_OCR=true + the org allowlist. The parked files drain themselves.
  3. Three empty brains on 349 packages. Cheapest first: teach the runtime-brain snapshot to union temporary_memories.
  4. Switch sales on for this tenant. Its plays already produce the highest utilities here, and this is a founder's fundraising inbox.
  5. Read the 16 deferrals before touching the floor. A floor nothing trips and a floor everything trips are both wrong; 16 of 33 is neither.
  6. 367 emails dropped at the gate. Correct for newsletters — N-06 is Gmail's own classifier and takes a first-time vendor with it.
  7. 69 events kept at unknown authority. The relevance model was refused because the ambiguous share was over 10% — a graph-coverage symptom, not a relevance one.

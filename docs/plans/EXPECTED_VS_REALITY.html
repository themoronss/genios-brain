GeniOS · Design Globe vs the first live run · 8 Sep 2026

Expected vs Reality

The design document said eight layers, 219 components and six Admin surfaces reachable on two connectors. The first live tenant produced nine cards, three of which are the same marketing email rendered as the founder's own overdue promise. This page puts the two side by side, layer by layer, and names the units responsible.

849 objects in 9 cards out 18/231 situations admitted 0/159 attachments read 1 of 4 brains speaking code acb18c8
00 · The answer in one paragraph

Nothing hallucinated. Four defensible decisions compose into a wrong product.

Layer 1 has no feature that distinguishes a message addressed to you from a message blasted to a list, so cold marketing enters as ordinary business mail. Layer 2 builds commitment_overdue without reading the owns edge it just wrote, so somebody else's promise is rendered in the founder's voice. Layer 2's admission gate requires a quoted span, which an absence can never have, so the 114 situations describing the real inbox work are held forever. Layer 3's Admin corpus is authored against facts nothing emits, so the one activated domain cannot fire and the general fallback copy renders what is left.

The compound effect

Spam surviving would be tolerable if the real work were arriving too — it would rank below it. What happened is that the genuine inbox work was held at the gate while the marketing mail sailed through. The noise is not merely present; it is over-represented by construction.

ExpectedRules first, model second; drops are logged, never silent; importance is deterministic and LLM-free.
RealityExactly that. 367 dropped on markers (N-02 198 · N-06 87 · N-03 82), 63 by the model gate, and every drop carries a reason code. ALG-17 is table-driven, integer, clock-free.
L1.4The missing unit — audience fan-outnot in code, not in the spec
ExpectedNothing. The Globe never assigns the question “was this addressed to me, or blasted to a list?” to any layer.
RealityTo and Cc are captured in full and persisted as source_events.recipients. Four modules read that column — the extraction prompt, the publisher record, the document register, support situations. No gate, scorer or situation rule reads the fan-out. A mail to one person and a mail to four hundred are the same message to every decision in this pipeline.

And the ladders do not compensate

Mid-band on authority, low-but-never-zero on criticality. The fix is one derived column and one weighted term — not a model.

The extractor was right

On the email that became the top card the model returned actor = "HealthX Elevate program", beneficiary = "selected applicants". It attributed the promise away from the tenant. Layer 1's three real failures are different in kind: absence (no documents readable), blindness (no fan-out feature) and compression (nothing scored above 4,640 of 10,000, because the money term carries the largest weight and this inbox has no amounts).

The single most important line in this analysis

The Sales corpus is substrate-aligned: it asks only for facts the engine writes. The Admin corpus asks for approval.state, filing.due_at, invoice.amount, asset.custodian, contract.end_at and fifteen more that nothing emits, plus twenty situation types that nothing mints. On a tenant where admin is the only activated domain, the Admin lane is structurally silent — and general_v1's relationship-hygiene copy renders the cards instead. That is not mis-tuning. It is a knowledge base pointed at a substrate that has not been built.

packs/admin_v1.py is 128 lines against sales_v1.py's 712, with "rules": [] and "plays": {} — and its own docstring gives this exact reason. The pack grants authority and declares vocabulary. It does not pretend to reason.

05 · Layer 4 · Reasoning

The most faithfully built layer in the system

All 17 reasoning units exist as modules, alongside the orchestrator, the evidence layer and a single decision_maker — exactly as the Globe specifies. 33 runs completed: 17 decided at 5000 bp, 16 deferred at 2300–3300.

Why half the runs deferthe guard working

The floor is DEFAULT_CONFIDENCE_FLOOR_BP = 4500. The binding input is Layer 2's own per-axis confidence, taken as a ceiling: on a subject known from one email and one source, evidence_score(1, 1) = 33 → 3300 → refused. An engine that speaks confidently about a single unconfirmed email is precisely what the floor exists to stop. The deferrals are an L1/L2 coverage symptom surfacing at L4, not an L4 defect.

The formula moves — but a third of it is constant4,000 bp idle

RANKING_WEIGHTS_V2 = importance 2500 · impact 2000 · urgency 2000 · success 1500 · effort 1000 · risk 1000, and it resolved 41 distinct utilities across 106 candidates — genuinely discriminating, unlike the v1 constant it replaced. But impact, risk and opportunity remain known constants.

Sales plays top the board on an admin-only tenanta Layer 3 symptom

The highest utilities come from usage_triggered_upsell (6643), adjacent_problem_cross_sell (6545) and renewal_starts_at_day_one (6475). Read §04 again: the Sales corpus is the only substrate-aligned one, so it is the only one that can score. Ranking is reporting the knowledge base's shape accurately.

10 · Order of work

What to do, and in which order

P0Ownership

Read the owns edge in read_overdue_commitments; branch the copy in general_v1. Smallest diff in this document; removes the worst card in the product.

P1Audience fan-out

Derive audience_size from the column already stored, add one S1 rule for a large external fan-out with no prior relationship, and one ALG-17 term. This is the missing unit the Globe never specified, and the reason promotional mail passes.

P2Absence receipts

Unblocks 114 situations — the two commonest types in any inbox. Until this lands, Follow-up Intelligence does not exist as a product.

P3OCR

The image is built. Deploy, flip the env, and 159 parked files drain themselves.

P4Corpus vs substrate

Decide explicitly: write the L2 producers for approval.*, filing.*, invoice.*, asset.* — or re-author the Admin corpus onto the 136 fact paths that exist. And switch sales on for this tenant now: it is a founder's fundraising inbox and the sales corpus is the substrate-aligned one.

P5Re-measure

python scripts/pipeline_funnel_report.py --org <org> after each. Every number here came from that command or from grep over the repository.

11 · Back-pressure on the design doc

Five corrections the Globe itself should absorb

  1. “Six surfaces fully reachable on two connectors” → one and a half, measured.
  2. The audience fan-out unit is missing from the architecture, not merely from the code. No layer in the Globe owns the question “addressed to me, or blasted to a list?”
  3. Rule 04 needs an absence clause. As written it makes silence unpublishable — and silence is the most common finding in an inbox.
  4. Domain knowledge needs a substrate contract. A capability reading a fact with no writer should fail authoring, not compile silently into a package that can never fire.
  5. The worked example needs a connector precondition. The AWS renewal trace requires finance and chat sources that are not buildable today.
